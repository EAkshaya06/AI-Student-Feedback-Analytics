Paste comments from students. Each non-empty line is analyzed as one comment.

No account or teacher email is needed to analyze pasted comments. Add an email only if you want to label the teacher.

CSV and TSV files can contain multiple columns; TXT files use one comment per line. Files are read in your browser. Review the comments below before previewing or submitting.

Anonymous by design: student names and email addresses are not requested or stored. Teacher email is optional; student names and emails are not collected.
